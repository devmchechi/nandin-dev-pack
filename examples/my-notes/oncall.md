# On-call

Last updated: 2026-09-28

- Rotation: weekly, Monday 10:00 to Monday 10:00, PagerDuty schedule "platform-primary".
- Handover happens at the Monday ops review. The outgoing engineer writes a handover note in #platform-oncall: open incidents, noisy alarms, anything paused.
- Swaps: agree with the other engineer, then update PagerDuty yourself and tell me.
- Sev 1 (customers affected): page me and post in #incidents within 15 minutes.
- After every Sev 1 or Sev 2: a written review within 5 working days. I review it.
